Privacy Policy

App: Firades  |  Last updated: July 1, 2025

This Privacy Policy explains how Firades ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the Firades mobile application (the "App"). By using the App you agree to the practices described below.

1. Information We Collect

We collect two categories of information:

a) Information you provide

  • Fitness preferences and goals you enter during onboarding (e.g. workout type, experience level, available time)
  • Workout and habit data you log inside the App

b) Information collected automatically

  • Device identifiers — used by Firebase to distinguish devices (not linked to your real identity)
  • App usage data — screens visited, features used, session length, crash reports (via Firebase Analytics)
  • Push notification tokens — generated when you allow notifications, used solely to deliver reminders and updates to your device

We do not collect your name, email address, payment information, location, contacts, camera, or microphone data.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide, personalize, and improve the App experience
  • To send push notifications you have opted into (workout reminders, habit nudges)
  • To understand how features are used so we can fix bugs and improve the App
  • To maintain the stability and security of the App

We do not sell your data to third parties or use it for advertising targeting.

3. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services that may collect data as described in their own privacy policies:

  • Google Firebase (Analytics, Cloud Messaging) — firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Expo (push notification infrastructure) — expo.dev/privacy

4. Data Storage

Your fitness preferences, workout history, and habit data are stored locally on your device using secure on-device storage. This data does not leave your device unless you explicitly share it.

Analytics and crash data collected by Firebase are stored on Google's servers in accordance with Google's data retention policies.

5. Push Notifications

If you allow notifications, we send workout reminders and habit nudges to your device. You can disable notifications at any time in your device's Settings app. Disabling notifications does not affect your ability to use any other part of the App.

6. Children's Privacy

Firades is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt out of analytics data collection (you can reset your device's advertising identifier in your device settings)

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the address below.

8. Data Retention

On-device data is retained until you delete the App. Firebase Analytics data is retained for 14 months by default, after which it is automatically deleted.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
